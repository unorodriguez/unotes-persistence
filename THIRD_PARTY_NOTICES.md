# Third-party scope and notices

The distributed library has no third-party runtime dependencies and does not bundle the development toolchain. No Google-origin proxy code, font, icon, image or full-application notice bundle is incorporated. Browser APIs are platform interfaces, not bundled third-party implementations.

Development and test dependencies retain their own licenses. `package-lock.json` identifies exact resolutions/integrity; `docs/DEPENDENCY_LICENSES.json` inventories the locked development closure. Installed packages retain their upstream LICENSE/NOTICE material. Where npm distributions omit top-level license texts, the reviewed supplemental evidence is documented separately. Do not redistribute node_modules, native binaries or a tooling/container image on the assumption that this notice licenses them; preserve that distribution's actual license obligations.

The source-provenance review found no identified external implementation incorporated into the selected project-authored boundary. This is bounded evidence, not a universal originality or non-infringement guarantee. OpenAI's code-generation terms warn that generated code can be subject to third-party licenses. Any later identified upstream material must be investigated and attributed under its actual terms.
